A ritual held every 1000 years by all within [[Gaelith]]; with every nation summoning forth their most brutal, heroic and noblest warriors to send off into the [[Astral Realm]]. Some channel the magic of the forest while others may weave the magic web so beautifully it could make a grown [Lich](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195106-lich) cry. And there are those who tear through flesh and bone to bathe in fierce and evil power of [[Blood Magic]]. Or incessant holy prayers with faith to become the Patron of one's God. The results are all the same: the warriors never return. 

Participating in this ritual is seen as the highest honor in all of [[Gaelith]], for if you are sent off you are never to return. As such months leading up to the ritual are seen as sacred and therefore blasphemous to intervene in for millennia. However in the [[Age Of The Ungodly]] the tides are turning with more and more non-believers appearing.
